How to Play

Create words by placing the white tiles from your rack onto the board. You cannot move black tiles.

Z10
H4
A1
C3
A1
T1

All words must be connected to each other and be a valid word vertically and horizontally.

Words are scored using the tile value in the bottom right corner of each tile.

Q10

Every day the word-brained solver bot will complete the puzzle to give you a target score.

98/105

The solver score isn't perfect. It can be beaten.

Letters placed on bonus tiles increase the score of either the individual tile value (2L, 3L) or the whole word (2W, 3W).

A bonus tile can be used on multiple words.

2L
3L
2W
3W

Using all your tiles awards you +50.
Using all your tiles on a single word awards you +100.

ALL TILE BONUS+50
BINGO!+100

Word List

The word list is made up of a combination of the ENABLE 2K Word List, the most common words for reading levels A0–C2 as listed on Lingvoberry, and a few "gotcha" words (QADI, ZA, QAT, etc.).

If you want to suggest an addition to the word list you can fill out the request form here.

Rules

Every day a randomised board of words and bonus squares is generated.

Make as many words as possible using the 7 tiles at the bottom of the board.

Bonus Tiles (Bonuses can be used by multiple words)

  • 2xL: Doubles the letter value of the tile placed on this tile
  • 3xL: Triples the letter value of the tile placed on this tile
  • 2xW: Doubles the score of the entire word placed on this tile
  • 3xW: Triples the score of the entire word placed on this tile

Using all 7 tiles gives you an additional +50 points

Using all 7 tiles on one word gives you an additional +100 points

How to Play

Create words by placing the white tiles from your rack onto the board.

You can drag tiles, click on the tile and where you want it to go or click on a board space and then letter you want to place there. You cannot move black tiles.

All words must be connected to each other and be a valid word vertically and horizontally.

Scoring

Words are scored using the tile value in the bottom right corner of each tile. Every day the Toucanagram solver bot will complete the puzzle which you to give you a target score.

The solver score isn't perfect. It can be beaten.

Bonuses

Letters placed on bonus tiles increase the score of either the individual tile value (2L, 3L) or the whole word (2W, 3W).

A bonus tile can be used multiple times on multiple words.
